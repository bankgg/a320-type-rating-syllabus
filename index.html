Course overview

Trainee A emphasis

Managed climb and FMGC work, APU MEL and crossbleed starts, fuel-feed and IDG faults, ADR/IR faults, dual-engine failure, overweight approaches, and severe engine damage.

Trainee B emphasis

Ground-speed mini and braking, start-valve and smoke events, FMGC resets, sidestick priority, emergency electrical configuration, contaminated-runway and gear work, and gravity gear extension.

Common ground

Both cover takeoffs, approaches, landings, stalls, UPRT and one-engine-inoperative handling. FFS 8 has the same UPRT exercise sequence; FFS 10 has the same skill-test exercise sequence, with different weather.

MFTD/FBS 2Packs OFF and managed-climb/FMGC work for A; packs ON for B. MFTD/FBS 3APU MEL and crossbleed start for A; ground-speed mini and braking for B. MFTD/FBS 4Fuel-feed/IDG/relight work for A; FMGC/FPV/cabin-pressure work for B. MFTD/FBS 5Hot start, cargo smoke and evacuation for A; engine/hydraulic/braking failures for B. MFTD/FBS 6Unreliable speed, ADR and fuel leak for A; start-valve and smoke for B. MFTD/FBS 7A adds relight/RNP and V2 takeoff failure; B adds fire and evacuation. MFTD/FBS 8A focuses on OEI approaches and RTO; B on FMGC resets and emergency electrical. MFTD/FBS 9Routes run in opposite directions; A focuses on engine fire/OEI, B on contamination and gear. FFS 1A introduces RMP/ACP; B adds sidestick priority. Handling exercises overlap. FFS 2A adds 3D RNP; both cover stalls, visual circuits and 2D RNP. FFS 3A adds cold-weather preflight/taxi; arrival handling largely overlaps. FFS 4IR faults and suspected damage for A; ADR faults and no-damage descent for B. FFS 5Dual-engine failure/RNP for A; unreliable speed/manual ILS for B. Alternate branch depends on crew roles. FFS 6A adds G+B hydraulics, flaps fault and RNP; B adds slats fault and gravity gear. FFS 7Opposite routes; A focuses on FCU/overweight/severe damage, B on windshear/fuel leak/APU fire. FFS 8The UPRT exercise sequence is the same for both trainees. FFS 9Opposite routes; EDTO failures differ at EEP, ETOPS entry and ETP. FFS 10The skill-test exercise sequence matches; departure and arrival weather differ.
