# FLIGHT CREW INCAPACITATION — handling plan (HS-TX)

> Study aid built from uncontrolled copies of the THA A320/A321 FCOM, FCTM and QRH (14 JUL 26 edition) and the Airbus PDP tutorials (Jan 2024) in `books/`. Not for operational use. Generated 2026-10-31 with the `a320-ecam-abnormal` skill.

**Technique · no memory items**  
**Applicability:** all HS-TX — Applicable to ALL (FCTM technique).

The fit pilot takes over with "I HAVE CONTROL" and secures the flight path. If the incapacitated pilot interferes with the controls, press the sidestick pb for 40 s. Declare the emergency to ATC, call the purser to help contain the incapacitated pilot, reduce the workload, and land at the nearest suitable airport with medical assistance arranged.

## 1. What it is
- **Trigger:** No ECAM alert. Absence of standard callouts at the appropriate time, a crewmember reporting feeling unwell, incoherent speech, a pale and(or) fixed facial expression, or irregular breathing. [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 9/32, PDF 454; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 518]
- **Flight phase inhibition:** none [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 9/32, PDF 454; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 518]
- Incapacitation is a real safety hazard that occurs more frequently than many other emergencies. It ranges from sudden death to partial loss of function, sometimes without any symptom beforehand. [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 9/32, PDF 454]
- Definition: any condition which affects the health of a crew member during the performance of duties which renders him incapable of performing the assigned duties. [PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 517]
- Pressing the sidestick pb gives priority to that sidestick: the aural "PRIORITY LEFT" sounds on a captain takeover, and the other sidestick is inoperative. [PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 520]

## 2. Key limits
- **Sidestick pb:** 40 s, if the controls are interfered with [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 520]
- **Landing:** Nearest suitable airport [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 521]

## 3. Flow (PF / PM)
### 1. Detect
**1.1 PF + PM**
- Routine monitoring and aural crosschecks: absence of a standard callout may mean incapacitation [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 9/32, PDF 454; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 518]
- If you do not feel well, tell the other crewmember [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 9/32, PDF 454]
- Other clues: no appropriate response to a verbal communication, incoherent speech, strange behavior, irregular breathing, pale fixed facial expression, jerky motions [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 9/32, PDF 454; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 518]
**1.2 PF + PM**
- During takeoff the PM must be ready to take over [PDP Low visibility operations, PDF 379]

### 2. Take over
**2.1 PF**
- The fit pilot is the PF from here [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 519]
- “I HAVE CONTROL” [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 519]
- **If the incapacitated pilot interferes with the handling of the aircraft** [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 519]
  - Press and keep pressed the sidestick pb for 40 s [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 519; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 520]
  - The 40 s includes the time to deactivate the AP (if engaged) and the offside sidestick [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455]
- Keep or engage the onside AP, as required [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 519]
- Perform callouts (challenge and response included) and checklists aloud [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 519]

### 3. Inform and contain
**3.1 PF**
- “MAYDAY, MAYDAY, MAYDAY” [PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 519]
- On the radio, to inform ATC of the emergency [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 519]
- “PURSER TO COCKPIT, PLEASE!” [FCOM PRO-ABN-ABN-00 P 9/12, PDF 2832]
- On the PA [FCOM PRO-ABN-ABN-00 P 9/12, PDF 2832]
- Take any steps possible to contain the incapacitated pilot; these may involve the cabin crew [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 519]

### 4. Reduce workload and land
**4.1 PF**
- Consider early approach preparation and checklists reading, automatic landing, and radar vectoring with a long approach [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 521]
- Land at the nearest suitable airport after consideration of all pertinent factors [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 521]
- Arrange medical assistance onboard and after landing, with as many details as possible about the condition [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 521]
- Request assistance from any medically qualified passenger (not on a flight with only two flight crewmembers onboard) [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 521]

## 7. FCTM technique
- The decision to reject the takeoff and the stop action are the responsibility of the Captain. The "STOP" call both confirms the decision and states that the Captain now has control; it is the only hand-over of control not accompanied by "I have control". [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 19/32, PDF 464]

## 10. Gaps and omissions
- The manuals have no specific procedure for incapacitation during a rejected takeoff, including who continues the stop if the Captain is the one incapacitated. The brief follows the general rule that the fit pilot takes over, confirmed by the user. [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455; FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 19/32, PDF 464]
- The PA wording differs: FCOM "PURSER TO COCKPIT, PLEASE!", PDP "ATTENTION, PURSER TO COCKPIT PLEASE". The brief uses the FCOM wording, confirmed by the user. [FCOM PRO-ABN-ABN-00 P 9/12, PDF 2832; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 519]
- The FCTM says press the sidestick pb "for 40 seconds"; the PDP says "at least 40 seconds". The brief uses the FCTM wording, confirmed by the user. [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455; PDP Crew incapacitation, PDF 519]
