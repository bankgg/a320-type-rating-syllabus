# HANDLING THE AIRCRAFT IN THE CASE OF SEVERE DAMAGE — handling plan (HS-TX)

> Study aid built from uncontrolled copies of the THA A320/A321 FCOM, FCTM and QRH (14 JUL 26 edition) and the Airbus PDP tutorials (Jan 2024) in `books/`. Not for operational use. Generated 2026-10-31 with the `a320-ecam-abnormal` skill.

**Technique · no memory items**  
**Applicability:** all HS-TX — Applicable to ALL (FCTM technique).

Fly the aircraft first. Bank, pitch and thrust are the primary parameters, controlled manually, while navigation and communication continue. Assess the handling qualities as soon as possible with smooth sidestick inputs and no more than 15° of bank, and avoid the speedbrakes unless necessary.

## 1. What it is
- **Trigger:** Severe damage to the aircraft, in particular if it significantly affects aerodynamics, flight controls or engines. [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455]
- **Flight phase inhibition:** none [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455]
- If the damage significantly affects aircraft aerodynamics, flight controls, or engines, then aircraft handling qualities may be affected. [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455]

## 2. Key limits
- **Bank during assessment:** Limit to 15° [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455]
- **Speedbrakes:** Avoid before the end of the flight, unless necessary [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455]

## 3. Flow (PF / PM)
### 1. Fly
**1.1 PF**
- Fly the aircraft [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455]
- Revert to "back to basics" flying if necessary: bank, pitch and thrust are the primary parameters to control manually [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455]
**1.2 PF + PM**
- Continue all navigation and communication tasks [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455]

### 2. Assess handling qualities
**2.1 PF**
- As soon as possible, identify how pitch, roll and yaw are controllable [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455]
- Apply smooth sidestick inputs and limit the bank angle to 15° [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455]
- This prevents possible destabilization of the aircraft [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455]
- Avoid the speedbrakes before the end of the flight, unless necessary [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 10/32, PDF 455]
