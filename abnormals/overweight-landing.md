# OVERWEIGHT LANDING — handling plan (HS-TX)

> Study aid built from uncontrolled copies of the THA A320/A321 FCOM, FCTM and QRH (14 JUL 26 edition) and the Airbus PDP tutorials (Jan 2024) in `books/`. Not for operational use. Generated 2026-10-14 with the `a320-ecam-abnormal` skill.

**QRH procedure · no memory items**  
**Applicability:** HS-TXA..TXP, HS-TXQ..TXU — QRH/FCOM procedure and FCTM technique apply to all HS-TX; only the maximum landing weight differs.

After the engine fire the aircraft must land soon, above its maximum landing weight. This is permitted only with the QRH OVERWEIGHT LANDING procedure. Plan a long, early-stabilised approach and use flaps FULL unless performance limits it. Turn the packs OFF (or supply them from the APU) for go-around thrust. Cross the threshold at VLS and touch down below 360 ft/min, then use max reverse early.

## 1. What it is
- **Trigger:** Immediate landing above maximum landing weight (in-flight turn back or diversion). [FCOM LIM-AG-WGHT P 1/2, PDF 4654; FCOM PRO-ABN-MISC P 21/30, PDF 3816]
- **Flight phase inhibition:** none [FCOM LIM-AG-WGHT P 1/2, PDF 4654; FCOM PRO-ABN-MISC P 21/30, PDF 3816]
- In exceptional cases (in-flight turn back or diversion), an immediate landing above maximum landing weight is permitted, provided the pilot follows the overweight landing procedure. [FCOM LIM-AG-WGHT P 1/2, PDF 4654]
- Above MLW, structural considerations require the ability to touch down at 360 ft/min without damage. [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 16/32, PDF 461]

## 2. Key limits
- **[HS-TXA..TXP]** **Max landing weight:** 64 500 kg [FCOM LIM-AG-WGHT P 1/2, PDF 4654]
- **[HS-TXQ..TXU]** **Max landing weight:** 66 000 kg [FCOM LIM-AG-WGHT P 1/2, PDF 4654]
- **Speed at runway threshold:** VLS [FCOM PRO-ABN-MISC P 21/30, PDF 3816; QRH 25.07A, PDF 225]
- **Max touchdown V/S:** 360 ft/min [FCOM PRO-ABN-MISC P 21/30, PDF 3816; FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 16/32, PDF 461]
- **Brake temperature:** be prepared for tire deflation above 800 °C [FCOM PRO-ABN-MISC P 21/30, PDF 3816]

## 3. Flow (PF / PM)
### 1. Decide and order
**1.1 PF**
- Exceptional case (turn back or diversion): immediate landing above MLW is permitted only with this procedure [FCOM LIM-AG-WGHT P 1/2, PDF 4654]
- “OVERWEIGHT LANDING PROCEDURE” [PDP Management of abnormal operations, PDF 427]
**1.2 PM**
- READ AND DO the QRH lines aloud; read the notes before the action they relate to [PDP Management of abnormal operations, PDF 427; FCTM AOP-30-30 P 5/6, PDF 86]
- FLAP LVR FULL FOR LANDING …… UNLESS SPECIFIED BY ABN PROC OR LIMITED BY LANDING PERF [QRH 25.07A, PDF 225]
- LDG DIST PROC …… APPLY [QRH 25.07A, PDF 225]
**1.3 PF + PM**
- Landing performance decides the configuration: below the max weight for go-around CONF 3 → land CONF FULL (go-around CONF 3); above it → land CONF 3 (go-around CONF 1+F) [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 16/32, PDF 461]

**2.1 PM**
- **For approach** [QRH 25.07A, PDF 225]
  - PACK 1 …… OFF OR SUPPLIED BY APU [QRH 25.07A, PDF 225]
  - PACK 2 …… OFF OR SUPPLIED BY APU [QRH 25.07A, PDF 225]
  - Increases maximum thrust available for a go-around [FCOM PRO-ABN-MISC P 21/30, PDF 3816]

**2.2 PF**
- Brief: long straight-in or wide visual pattern; early stabilised approach with VAPP at the FAF [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 16/32, PDF 461]
- Go-around: transition from −3° to climb uses a lot of energy and some height; follow SRS up to the displayed VLS [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 16/32, PDF 461]

### 3. Approach
**3.1 PF**
- At high weight VFE NEXT may be below green dot / S: select speed on the FCU to decelerate to VFE NEXT [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 16/32, PDF 461]
- If VFE CONF 1 is below VLS: disconnect A/THR to decelerate to VFE CONF 1; re-engage and use managed speed once in CONF 1 or 2 [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 16/32, PDF 461]
- Reduce speed to reach VLS at the threshold (manual landing and autoland) [FCOM PRO-ABN-MISC P 21/30, PDF 3816; FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 16/32, PDF 461]

### 4. Landing
**4.1 PF**
- INCREASE FLARE HEIGHT [QRH 25.07A, PDF 225]
- Touch down smoothly and symmetrically; V/S at most 360 ft/min [FCOM PRO-ABN-MISC P 21/30, PDF 3816]
- USE MAX REVERSE ASAP [QRH 25.07A, PDF 225]
- **After nosewheel touchdown** [QRH 25.07A, PDF 225]
  - APPLY BRAKES AS NECESSARY — use the maximum available runway length to limit braking energy [QRH 25.07A, PDF 225; FCOM PRO-ABN-MISC P 21/30, PDF 3816]
**4.2 PM**
- **When landing completed** [QRH 25.07A, PDF 225]
  - BRAKE FANS …… ON [QRH 25.07A, PDF 225]
- “OVERWEIGHT LANDING PROCEDURE COMPLETED” [PDP Management of abnormal operations, PDF 427]
**4.3 PF + PM**
- V/S above 360 ft/min at touchdown requires a maintenance inspection [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 16/32, PDF 461]

### [QRH] OVERWEIGHT LANDING (QRH 25.07A / FCOM PRO-ABN-MISC)

  - PACK 1 …… OFF OR SUPPLIED BY APU [QRH 25.07A, PDF 225]
  - PACK 2 …… OFF OR SUPPLIED BY APU — Increases the maximum thrust available in the case of a go-around. [QRH 25.07A, PDF 225]
  - **If landing CONF other than FULL: USE CONF 1+F FOR GO AROUND** [QRH 25.07A, PDF 225]
  - *SPEED AT RUNWAY THRESHOLD : VLS* [QRH 25.07A, PDF 225; FCOM PRO-ABN-MISC P 21/30, PDF 3816]
  - *MINIMIZE V/S AT TOUCHDOWN* [QRH 25.07A, PDF 225; FCOM PRO-ABN-MISC P 21/30, PDF 3816]
- **For landing:** [QRH 25.07A, PDF 225]
  - *INCREASE FLARE HEIGHT* [QRH 25.07A, PDF 225]
  - *USE MAX REVERSE ASAP* [QRH 25.07A, PDF 225]

## 7. FCTM technique
- Fly a long straight-in approach or a wide visual pattern to configure early; VAPP at the FAF, VLS at the threshold. [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 16/32, PDF 461]
- VLS CONF 1+F may be higher than VLS CONF 3 + 5 kt in a CONF 1+F go-around: follow SRS, which accelerates to the displayed VLS. [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 16/32, PDF 461]
- Modulate braking with the runway available to avoid very hot brakes and tire deflation. [FCTM PR-AEP-MISC P 16/32, PDF 461]

## 10. Gaps and omissions
